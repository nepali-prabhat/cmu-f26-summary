Course policies — late days, grading, AI use

As written in each course's syllabus, read on Aug 25, 2026. Syllabi get amended — check Canvas or ask the instructor before relying on any of this for a real deadline.

15-440Distributed Systems
Late days

10 late days total for the semester, shared across all projects and homework. Up to 3 per single deadline with no grade penalty. Nothing is accepted more than 72 hours (3 days) after a due date, and TA help stops at the official deadline. On partnered projects, late days are drawn from both partners' balances. The one exception is a serious, advisor-verified medical emergency — contact the instructors directly.

Grading

Projects 36% (P0 5, P1 8, P2 5, P3 18) · Written HW 5% · Quizzes 15% · Midterm 20% · Final 20% · Participation 4%. You must independently pass all three components — homework, quizzes/exams, and projects — to pass the course.

AI policy

Projects: AI tools (including agents) are allowed, but you must understand every line and be able to produce a similar solution on paper without AI. Homework: strongly recommended by hand, AI only as a high-level resource. Quizzes, exams, and presentations: no AI or outside resources at all. Any AI use on a project or homework must be disclosed — the tool/model, how it was used, and what for — and course material can't be fed into a platform whose retention or training policy could leak it.

Source: course website syllabus

14-642Embedded Systems
Late days

No fixed late-day bank — it's negotiable. Contact Patrick Tague as soon as you know you'll be late, ideally before the deadline. The syllabus itself says late submissions are still accepted through the last day of class, with any grade penalty left to the instructor's discretion, and that TA help isn't guaranteed once a deadline has passed.

Grading

Lab exercises 75% · Quizzes 25%. Labs may be done with a partner over a private GitHub repo in the cmu14642 org; quizzes are online/asynchronous and must be completed entirely solo.

AI policy

Generative AI is allowed only for basic supporting tasks on labs (e.g. syntax lookups), and every use must be cited — the tool, your exact prompt, and the response you got. AI is prohibited on quizzes outright, and any other use counts as cheating. Course materials (slides, assignments, quiz questions) may not be shared with AI tools at all — that's a copyright violation on top of an integrity one.

Source: PDF syllabus, uploaded Aug 25, 2026

15-618Parallel Computer Architecture
Late days

5 late-day points for the semester, usable on programming assignments only (not quizzes or the final project). A solo assignment costs 1 point/day late; a 2-person team costs 2 points/day (1 from each partner). Once points are gone, late hand-ins lose 10%/day, capped at 3 days late — nothing is accepted after that.

Grading

Programming assignments 25% · two exams 40% (20% each, no final exam) · written assignments + quizzes 10% · final project 25%. Cutoffs: 90 A · 80 B · 70 C · 60 D, with room for borderline bumps based on attendance, participation, and improvement.

AI policy

Varies by assignment, but document all AI use regardless. Conceptual questions: fine for linking topics together. Written assignments: minimize use, no copy/paste in or out, and AI is banned outright during peer grading. Programming assignment 1: no AI tools at all. Assignments 2–4: AI is OK only for small-scale code generation (e.g. a linked list) and framework/API lookups — not for designing the actual parallel algorithm. Reports: AI can help with LaTeX, formatting, and grammar, not rewriting whole paragraphs. Never use it to analyze results or draw conclusions.

Source: PDF syllabus, uploaded Aug 25, 2026

14-757ML with Adversaries in Mind
Late days

Each assignment can go up to 2 days (48 hours) late, drawn from a 7-late-day pool for the whole semester that's applied automatically. Once the pool is used up, late submissions lose 20 points/day. A genuine life-changing event is grounds for extra consideration — talk to Prof. Varodayan ahead of time if you can.

Grading

1050 points total: HW0 = 100, HW1–HW6 = 150 each, code discussions = 50 combined. Letter cutoffs: A 980 · A- 945 · B+ 910 · B 875 · B- 840 · C+ 805 · C 770 · C- 735 · D+ 700 · D 630. Regrade requests are due on Gradescope within 1 week of grades being returned.

AI policy

Generative AI is welcome, but every contribution needs a citation — quote AI text verbatim with a citation, cite paraphrased AI content, and cite non-text AI output (images, code, etc.) only where the assignment prompt expressly allows it. You still do your own research and build your own bibliographies, and passing off AI content as your own work is treated as plagiarism. You're responsible for anything inaccurate or biased that makes it into your submission.

Source: PDF syllabus, uploaded Aug 25, 2026
